We're launching this blog as a place to share how we think about **verifiable provenance**
and the cryptography that makes it possible without sacrificing privacy.

## What to expect

Expect a mix of the following:

- Research notes on zero-knowledge proof systems
- Engineering deep dives on the systems we build
- Perspective pieces on privacy, authenticity, and trust online

## Why provenance matters

As synthetic media becomes indistinguishable from the real thing, the ability to
_prove_ where a piece of content came from — without exposing the underlying data —
becomes foundational infrastructure for the open web.

> Trust should be verifiable, not assumed.

## Adding new posts

Every post here is just a Markdown file. Drop a new `.md` file into `content/blog`,
and it automatically shows up on this page. More on that soon.
