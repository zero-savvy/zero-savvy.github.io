What VIMz Solves

The digital landscape is under siege. Deepfakes flood social media, AI-generated content blurs reality, and platforms struggle to authenticate media without invading privacy.

The Trust Crisis
Platforms can't verify image authenticity without access to private data. Marketplaces lack cryptographic proof of provenance. Content creators face attribution challenges.
Privacy vs. Authentication
Traditional verification requires centralized platforms with access to original images. This creates privacy risks for journalists, activists, and sensitive organizations.
Marketplace Integrity
Digital rights marketplaces struggle with counterfeit content. NFT platforms lack verifiable provenance. Collectors have no way to prove authenticity.
Scalability Challenge
Existing solutions are slow, expensive, or centralized. Enterprise deployments require custom infrastructure. Open-source alternatives are fragmented.

How VIMz Works

A user-friendly protocol, complex cryptography with non-interactive and privacy-preserving proofs.

Step 1
Image Capture/Generation
The orginal high-resolution image gets captured and signed by an authentic camera (DSLR or App).
Step 2
Edits & Refinements
The user applies any desired (authentic) edit and transformation on the image.
Step 3
ZK Proof
VIMz Creates a zero-knowledge proof proving the resulting image's origin and provenance, while anonymizing the identity of both creator and editor.
Step 4
Verification
Anyone can verify authenticity in milliseconds without accessing the original image.

Anonymous Journalism

Why Zero-Knowledge Matters

Privacy Without Compromise

With VIMz, you prove an image is authentic without revealing the image itself. Journalists can authenticate leaked documents. Activists can prove incidents without endangering collaborators. Artists can claim ownership without public disclosure.

Trustless Verification

No central authority. No platform dependency. Verification works on-chain or off-chain. Anyone with the proof can verify instantaneously, making VIMz suitable for censorship-resistant applications and decentralized marketplaces.

Cryptographic Certainty

VIMz advanced folding-based zkSNARKs that are compact ("<11KB") and verify in milliseconds, making them suitable for resource-constrained environments and real-time verification.

Regulatory Alignment

GDPR-compliant by design. Privacy-enhancing technology (PET) meeting EU regulations. Suitable for sovereign systems, financial institutions, and privacy-regulated industries.
